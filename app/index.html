    How RxGap decides

    We use the Massachusetts Health Policy Commission’s method (DataPoints 31, October 2025), on census block groups.

    • Desert: less than 15% of the block group’s area is within the walk limit of an open pharmacy.
    • Walk limit: 1 mile, or ½ mile for low-access block groups (car ownership below the state average, or more than 20% of households in poverty).
    • One closure away: covered today, but losing one specific pharmacy makes it a desert.
