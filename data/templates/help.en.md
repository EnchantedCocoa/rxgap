## Get refills by mail

Call the member number on your health plan card. Ask: "Can you mail my daily medicines, {mail_months} months at a time?" Mail works best for medicine you take every day. It is not for antibiotics or medicine you need today.

## Pick up everything on one day

Ask your pharmacist: "Can you line up my refills so they are all ready on the same day?" One trip instead of many.

## A ride, for a vaccine

MassHealth can pay for a ride to a pharmacy for a vaccine. Only your doctor can ask for it, with a {pt1_form} form. It can take up to {pt1_days} business days. MassHealth does not pay for rides to pick up medicine ({pickup_rides_cite}). MassHealth Customer Service: {mh_phone}.
